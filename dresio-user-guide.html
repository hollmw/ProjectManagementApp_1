1.  Overview

DRESIO Workspace is a project and intern management platform built for organisations running structured internship programmes. It lets admins create projects, assign interns based on their business area and availability, track progress in real time, and analyse how well intern capacity is being used across the organisation.

The platform has two access levels:

  • Admins & Members — full access to all pages including user management, analytics, and the intern timeline.
  • Interns — read-only access to the task board, Gantt chart, and leaderboard. They cannot create tasks or manage users.
TIP
All pages are accessible from the sidebar on the left. Admins see an extra Admin section containing advanced tools.

4.  Task Board

The Task Board is the central hub of the platform. It shows all projects as cards organised into three columns.

ColumnMeaning
To DoTasks that have not yet been started.
In ProgressTasks actively being worked on.
DoneCompleted tasks.

4.1  Filtering Tasks

Above the board there is a row of filter controls:

  • Search — type any part of a task title to instantly filter the board.
  • Business Area — filter tasks by a specific area (Tech, Business, Marketing, Science, Clinical, Design).
  • Priority — filter by High, Medium, or Low priority.
  • Assigned to me — show only tasks you are personally assigned to.

4.2  Reading a Task Card

Each card on the board shows the following at a glance:

ElementDescription
Left colour stripA vertical stripe on the left edge. Each colour segment represents a business area the task belongs to. Multiple areas = multiple colours stacked vertically.
TitleThe name of the project.
Area chipsColoured pills showing which business areas the task belongs to (e.g. Tech, Clinical).
Priority badge↑ High (red), → Medium (amber), or ↓ Low (green).
Slot fill pillsSmall pills showing how many intern slots are filled per area. "Tech 1/3" means 1 of 3 Tech slots filled. Green ✓ = exactly met. Indigo ↑ = over-filled.
Due dateThe deadline for the task.
Status badgeShows the current status: todo, in_progress, done, or complete if all steps are checked.
Assigned avatarsCircular initials of everyone assigned. Up to 4 shown, then a +N overflow badge.

4.3  Creating a Task

Click the + New Task button (top right of the board). A modal will open with the following fields:

FieldDescription
TitleRequired. The project name.
DescriptionOptional. A short summary of what the project involves.
Business AreasRequired. Select one or more areas from the coloured pills. The first area selected becomes the primary area.
PriorityChoose High, Medium, or Low. Defaults to Medium.
Start DateWhen the project begins. Used for intern availability checking.
Due DateThe project deadline.
Slot RequirementsFor each business area selected, set how many interns from that area are needed using the + and − buttons.
Breakdown StepsAdd individual checklist items. Each step can be checked off by interns and earns 10 points on the leaderboard.

Click Save to create the task. It will appear in the To Do column.

4.4  Editing or Deleting a Task

Click the ⋯ menu (three dots) in the top-right corner of any task card:

  • Edit task — reopens the creation modal with all fields pre-filled.
  • Delete task — removes the task permanently after a confirmation prompt. Admin/member only.

5.  Assigning Interns to Tasks

The assignment panel lives at the bottom of each task card. It shows who is currently assigned and lets you add or remove people.

5.1  Opening the Assign Panel

Click the + Assign button on the right side of the Assigned row. The panel expands to show a list of all users.

5.2  Reading the User List

Each row in the panel shows one user with the following information:

  • Avatar initial — coloured by their matching business area if they qualify for a slot requirement; grey otherwise.
  • Name — faded and at 45% opacity if they do not match any slot requirement on this task.
  • Area chips — all business areas the intern belongs to. Coloured if the area matches a slot requirement; grey otherwise.
  • Busyness badge — shows concurrent projects during this task's date range. Green = free/1 project, Amber = 2 projects, Red = 3 or more.
  • ⚠ Starts late — appears if the intern's placement start date is after the task's start date.
  • ⚠ Ends early — appears if the intern's placement end date is before the task's due date.
  • Assign / Assigned ✓ button — click to toggle assignment on or off.

5.3  Filtering Users by Area

A row of area filter tabs appears above the user list. Click any area to show only users who belong to it. Areas with a slot requirement on this task are highlighted with a coloured dot (●). Click All to clear the filter.

5.4  Slot Status Indicators

At the top of the assignment panel, coloured pills show the fill status for each slot requirement:

  • Area colour (dim) — slots not yet filled. E.g. "Tech 1/3".
  • Green ✓ — all slots for that area are filled. E.g. "Tech 3/3 ✓".
  • Indigo ↑ — more people assigned than required. E.g. "Tech 4/3 ↑".

5.5  Auto-Fill

If a task has slot requirements set, an ⚡ Auto-fill button appears next to the + Assign button. Clicking it automatically assigns the best available interns based on the following rules:

RuleHow it works
Slot prioritySlots with the lowest fill rate are filled first — most understaffed slots get interns first.
Specialist firstInterns who belong to only one matching area are preferred over multi-discipline interns, preserving versatile interns as backups for other slots.
Least busy firstAmong equally qualified interns, those with the fewest concurrent projects during the task period are selected first.
Placement overlapOnly interns whose placement period fully covers the task's date range are eligible. Interns who start after the project starts or finish before it ends are automatically skipped.

After auto-fill completes, a feedback toast appears showing how many interns were assigned and how many were skipped (with the reason, e.g. "2 skipped — start after project").

TIP
Set both a Start Date and Due Date on the task, and make sure intern placement dates are configured in User Management, before using Auto-fill. Without dates the availability check cannot run.

6.  Task Breakdown Steps

Breakdown steps are individual checklist items within a task. They allow work to be tracked at a granular level and tie directly into the leaderboard points system.

  • Steps are listed below the task description inside the task card.
  • Any user assigned to the task can check off a step by clicking the checkbox.
  • Checking a step for the first time awards 10 points to the user who checked it.
  • Unchecking a step removes it from the activity log but does not deduct points.
  • When all steps are checked the task status badge changes to complete.
NOTE
Steps can only be added or edited when creating or editing a task via the ⋯ menu. They cannot be reordered after creation.

7.  Gantt Chart

The Gantt Chart provides a timeline view of all tasks. Tasks are shown as horizontal bars stretching from their start date to their due date.

8.1  Reading the Chart

  • Each row is one task.
  • Bars are coloured by the task's primary business area colour.
  • The vertical line marks today's date.
  • Hover over a bar to see the task title and dates in a tooltip.

8.2  Filtering by User

Use the User dropdown at the top of the page to filter the chart to tasks assigned to a specific person. This is especially useful for reviewing an individual intern's workload. The Intern Timeline page links directly to this view — clicking a user row there opens the Gantt pre-filtered to that person.

8.  Leaderboard

The Leaderboard ranks all users by the number of points they have earned. Points are awarded when a user checks off a breakdown step for the first time.

PointsWhen awarded
10 pointsAwarded per unique breakdown step checked for the first time.
0 pointsUnchecking a step, or re-checking a step already checked before, earns no points.

The leaderboard updates in real time as steps are checked. It is visible to all users including interns.

9.  User Management

Found under Admin → User Management. This page lets admins create, edit, and remove users, set their roles, assign them to business areas, and configure intern placement dates.

10.1  The User Table

All users are listed in a table. The table can be filtered using the controls above it:

  • Search — filter by name.
  • Business Area dropdown — show only users who belong to a specific area.
  • Show unassigned only — tick this checkbox to highlight interns who have not been assigned to any task yet. A red Unassigned badge appears next to any intern with zero task assignments.

10.2  Creating a User

Click + Add User. Fill in the following fields:

FieldDescription
Full NameThe user's display name (appears in avatars and assignments).
EmailUsed to log in. Must be unique.
PasswordInitial password. The user can change this after first login.
RoleAdmin (full access), Member (full access except user deletion), or Intern (read-only task board access).
Business AreasSelect one or more areas. These determine which slot requirements the user is eligible for.
Placement Start DateIntern only. The date the intern's programme begins.
Placement End DateIntern only. The date the intern's programme ends.
IMPORTANT
Placement dates are critical for the Auto-fill feature. Without them, interns cannot be matched to tasks by date range and will not appear as eligible candidates during auto-assignment.

10.3  Editing a User

Click the Edit button on any user row. All fields can be updated including placement dates and business areas. Changes take effect immediately.

10.4  Deleting a User

Click the Delete button on a user row. You will be asked to confirm. Deleting a user removes them from all task assignments — this action cannot be undone.

10.  Intern Timeline

Found under Admin → Intern Timeline. This is a resource Gantt chart showing every intern on the vertical axis and dates on the horizontal axis.

11.1  Reading the Timeline

  • Each row represents one user.
  • The dashed shaded band shows the intern's placement period (start to end date).
  • Solid coloured bars represent assigned tasks during that period.
  • A vertical line marks today.
  • Weekend columns are lightly shaded.

11.2  Bar Colour Coding

Task bars are coloured based on how many projects the intern is running concurrently at that time:

ColourMeaning
Green1 project running — comfortable workload.
Amber2 projects running concurrently — manageable but worth monitoring.
Red3 or more projects overlapping — intern is overloaded.

Bars outlined in amber with a ⚠ icon indicate tasks that extend beyond the intern's placement end date.

11.3  Interacting with the Timeline

  • Click any user row to open the Gantt Chart pre-filtered to that person.
  • Use the Show all users / Interns only toggle in the top-right to switch between views.
  • The timeline automatically scrolls to today on page load.
  • Hover over a task bar to see the title and any warnings in a tooltip.

11.4  Left Panel

The fixed left panel shows each user's name, their business area chips, and a red Unassigned badge if they have no tasks. Click anywhere on the row to navigate to their Gantt.

11.  Activity Log

Found under Admin → Activity Log. This page shows a chronological feed of all actions taken in the platform, most recent first.

Each entry shows the user who performed the action, a description of what was done, the task it relates to, any points awarded, and the timestamp. This is useful for auditing intern participation and tracking who completed which steps.

12.  User Analytics

Found under Admin → User Analytics. This dashboard provides a high-level view of how the organisation is performing across all projects and interns.

13.1  Summary Stat Cards

CardWhat it shows
Total UsersTotal number of accounts in the system.
Completion RatePercentage of filtered tasks that are fully completed.
Overdue TasksNumber of tasks past their due date that are not yet done.
Slot Fill RateOverall percentage of intern slots filled across all tasks with slot requirements.
Unassigned InternsNumber of interns with zero task assignments. Zero is shown in green.
Active TodayNumber of users who have logged any activity today.

13.2  Filters

  • Group by — switch between grouping metrics by User or by Business Area.
  • Business Area — filter all data to a single area.
  • User — filter all data to a single person.
  • Due from / Due to — filter tasks by due date range.
  • Clear filters — resets all filters at once.

13.3  Completion Chart

A horizontal bar chart showing completion rates. Each bar represents one user or area (depending on Group by). Bars show the proportion of done tasks (indigo) and overdue tasks (amber). The completion percentage is shown to the right.

13.4  Activity Trend

A line chart showing total activity events per day over the last 7, 14, 30, or 60 days. Use the dropdown in the chart header to change the time window. Filter by user to see an individual's activity curve.

13.5  Detailed Metrics Table

A sortable table with one row per user or area. Click any column header to sort. Columns include: Tasks, Done, Overdue, Rate (with mini progress bar), Steps, Steps Done, and Steps Late.

13.6  Slot Coverage by Area

For each business area with slot requirements, this section shows how many slots have been filled vs. how many are required in total across all tasks. The progress bar turns green when 100% filled.

13.7  Intern Capacity vs Demand

A dual-bar chart comparing the number of interns available in each area against the total number of intern slots demanded by all tasks. A ⚠️ warning icon appears when demand exceeds capacity.

13.8  Intern Utilisation

A bar per intern showing what percentage of their placement period is covered by at least one assigned task. Colour coded: Green ≥ 80% (well utilised), Amber 50–79% (moderate), Indigo <50% (under-utilised), Hatched = no placement dates set.

14.  Quick Reference

Business Areas

  • Tech — technology and software projects.
  • Business — commercial and operational projects.
  • Marketing — brand, content, and growth projects.
  • Science — research and data-driven projects.
  • Clinical — health and clinical research projects.
  • Design — UX, visual, and product design projects.

Task Priority Colours

PriorityDescription
↑ HighUrgent tasks that should be actioned immediately.
→ MediumStandard priority tasks.
↓ LowTasks that can be deferred without impact.

Intern Timeline Bar Colours

ColourMeaning
Green1 concurrent project — healthy workload.
Amber2 concurrent projects — monitor closely.
Red3+ concurrent projects — overloaded, consider reassignment.

Auto-fill Priority Order

  • Most understaffed slot filled first (lowest fill ratio).
  • Specialist interns preferred (single-area) over generalists.
  • Least busy intern selected when skills are equal.
  • Alphabetical as final tiebreaker.

Points System

  • 10 points per breakdown step checked for the first time.
  • No points for unchecking or re-checking an already-checked step.
